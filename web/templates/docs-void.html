The derived description

Where an endpoint publishes no description of itself, this service derives one from what it observed and serves it at {{ void_path }}?url=… as RDF. It is VoID, with a few terms of our own where VoID has none or where a number would otherwise claim more than it can.

The document describes OUR OBSERVATION and never the endpoint's own dataset: the endpoint is the object of void:sparqlEndpoint and prov:wasDerivedFrom and the subject of nothing in it.
